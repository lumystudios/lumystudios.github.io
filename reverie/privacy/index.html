Reverie Privacy Policy

Reverie — Mahjong Tiles · Effective November 1, 2026

Reverie is a relaxing mahjong tile-matching game developed by Udi Magen (“we”, “us”). There are no accounts, chat or social features, and we do not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or location.

Information stored on the device and in your iCloud

  • Your levels, stars, points, trophies, daily-puzzle streak and settings are saved on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • If you use iCloud, your progress (levels, stars, points, trophies and daily puzzles) is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it follows you to your other devices. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we can't see it. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
  • Deleting the app deletes the information on the device.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Reverie sends your total points, levels cleared, daily puzzles completed and trophies to Apple's Game Center for its leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is optional and run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive any of this information.

Advertising

Reverie is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. There are no ads in the first 10 levels; after that, a short ad plays before every third level, and you can choose to watch a video for stars. Ads never interrupt a board you are playing, and the optional “Remove Ads” purchase turns off the ads between levels.

To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect device information such as the device type, operating-system version, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • Apple's tracking prompt. When you first open Reverie, it asks whether you allow tracking, before any ads load. If you allow it, Google can use the advertising identifier to show ads that fit your interests. If you don't, you still see ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Reverie shows Google's consent form, and you can change your choices later in the app under Settings › Ads › Privacy choices for ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase and the star packs are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase went through.

Children

Reverie is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and information was collected, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our side. For data processed by Google to serve ads, use the in-app privacy choices, the iPhone tracking setting, or Google's privacy controls linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
