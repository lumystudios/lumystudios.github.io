Reverie Privacy Policy

Reverie — Mahjong Tiles · Effective October 10, 2026

Reverie is a relaxing mahjong tile-matching game developed by Udi Magen (“we”, “us”). There are no accounts, chat or social features, and we do not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or location.

Information stored on the device

  • Your levels, stars, points, trophies, daily-puzzle streak and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Deleting the app deletes this information.

Advertising

Reverie is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only after you choose a helper (such as Shuffle or Undo) or when you choose to watch a video for stars or a reward; no ad starts on its own during play.

To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect device information such as the device type, operating-system version, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • Apple's tracking prompt. After you have played a few levels, Reverie asks whether you allow tracking. If you allow it, Google can use the advertising identifier to show ads that fit your interests. If you don't, you still see ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Reverie shows Google's consent form, and you can change your choices later in the app under Settings › Ads › Privacy choices for ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchase

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is active.

Children

Reverie is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and information was collected, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our side. For data processed by Google to serve ads, use the in-app privacy choices, the iPhone tracking setting, or Google's privacy controls linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
