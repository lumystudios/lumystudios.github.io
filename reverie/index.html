Support

What happens when the tray is full?

Four tiles and no pair ends the board. Undo takes the last tile back, Retry starts the board again, or watch a short video for a second chance that sends three tiles back.

How do I get more stars?

Clear levels (1–3 stars each), finish the daily puzzle, open the chapter chest every 10 levels, or watch a short video when you need more.

What does Remove Ads do?

A one-time purchase that turns off the short ads after Shuffle, Undo and Retry. Videos for stars stay available and are always your choice. Use Settings › Restore Purchases on a new device.
