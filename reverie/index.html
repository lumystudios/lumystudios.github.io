Support

What happens when the tray is full?

Four tiles and no pair ends the board. Undo takes the last tile back, Retry starts the board again, or watch a short video for a second chance that sends three tiles back.

How do I get more stars?

Clear levels (1–3 stars each), beat hard levels for bonus stars, finish the daily puzzle, open the chapter chest every 10 levels, or watch a short video when you need more.

What does Remove Ads do?

A one-time purchase that turns off the short ads between levels. Videos for stars stay available and are always your choice. On a new device, open Settings on the home screen and tap Restore Purchases.

When do ads appear?

There are no ads in the first 10 levels. After that, a short ad plays before every third level. Ads never interrupt a board you are playing.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device and moves with an iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer. There is no account, so it can't be synced any other way.
