Website privacy policy

lumystudios.github.io · Effective November 3, 2026

This policy covers this website. Each app has its own privacy policy, listed below. The website is run by Udi Magen (“we”, “us”), the developer of the apps shown here.

The short version

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking on this website.
  • No forms and no accounts. We only learn about you if you email us.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from this website, not from other companies' servers.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub's servers technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. GitHub may log this to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't receive these logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Storage on your device

The website stores nothing on your device: no cookies, no local storage and no similar technology. That's why there is no cookie banner.

Email

If you email lumystudios@yahoo.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and fix the problem you describe. We keep the conversation while it's useful for helping you, then delete it, and we never add you to a mailing list. Our email is provided by Yahoo, which processes messages under the Yahoo Privacy Policy. If you are in the EU, the UK or a similar region, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your message, or steps you asked us to take.

Links to other websites

Pages link to other websites, such as the App Store, Google and Apple support pages. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us, write to us and we will delete the message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK or California, you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Because the website collects nothing, this only applies to emails you sent us. Write to us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell or share personal information, as those words are defined in California law.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
