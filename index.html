PencilPop

Color by number for iPhone and iPad

Match the numbers, fill the shapes and watch a picture come to life, one relaxing tap at a time.

  • 125 pictures on four levels, from simple scenes to mandalas
  • A Magic Wand that fills a whole area at once
  • Timelapse videos of every finished picture

Reverie

Mahjong tiles for iPhone

Calm tile matching on a journey through twenty hand-painted worlds.

  • 200 levels, with hard levels in every world
  • Every board can be cleared from the start
  • A daily puzzle and trophies to collect
See Reverie Coming soon to the App Store

Little Wonders

Match and build for iPhone

A cozy match-3 puzzle game. Win stars to build six tiny worlds, from a lighthouse in a bottle to a storybook woodland.

  • 180 levels across six little worlds
  • A shark that clears a row, a fishing line that clears a column
  • No ads between levels, only videos you choose
See Little Wonders Coming soon to the App Store

Starling

Clipboard manager for Mac

Everything you copy is saved in your menu bar: text, images and code, a keystroke away.

  • Search and label your clipboard history
  • Passwords and API keys are left out automatically
  • Nothing leaves your Mac
See Starling Coming soon to the Mac App Store
