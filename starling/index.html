Starling

Clipboard Manager · Mac

Everything you copy, right where you left it.

A lightweight clipboard manager that lives in your menu bar. Copy once, paste anytime — text, images, and code, always a keystroke away.

Clipboard history

Every copy is saved automatically. Scroll back through text and images, and paste any of them.

One keystroke

Press your hotkey and the overlay appears right where you are — even over full-screen apps.

Search

Start typing to filter your history instantly. Find that link, that snippet, that number.

Labels

Tag clips with colour-coded labels to keep them organised. Filter by label to find what you need.

Read aloud

Have any text clip read out loud, straight from the list.

Sensitive-data guard

Passwords, API keys, and tokens are detected and excluded from history automatically.

Privacy first

Your data never leaves your Mac. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

Lightweight

Sits quietly in the menu bar. No Dock icon, no windows in your way.

Support

How do I open the clipboard history?

Press Cmd+Shift+V (the default hotkey) to show the overlay. You can change the hotkey in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

All clipboard history is stored locally on your Mac inside the app's container. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Does Starling need Accessibility permission?

Yes — Starling uses it to paste clips into your active app. macOS will prompt you the first time. You can also grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Can I clear my history?

Yes. Open Settings and tap Clear History, or use the same option in the menu-bar menu.

Does it save images?

Yes. When image clipboard support is on (the default), copied images are saved alongside text clips.
