Starling Privacy Policy

Starling: Clipboard Manager for Mac · Effective October 3, 2026

Starling is a clipboard manager for macOS, developed by Udi Magen ("we", "us"). It is designed to keep your data on your device and collect nothing.

No data collection

Starling does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Information stored on your device

  • Clipboard history — text and images you copy are saved in a local database inside the app's sandboxed container. They never leave your Mac.
  • Settings — your preferences (hotkey, window position, labels, retention) are stored locally alongside the clipboard history.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Sensitive data

Starling automatically detects common secret formats (API keys, tokens, private keys, database connection strings) and excludes them from the clipboard history. It also respects the macOS concealed-clipboard flag used by password managers.

Accessibility permission

Starling requests the macOS Accessibility permission so it can paste clipboard items into the app you are working in. This permission is used only for that purpose — simulating a paste keystroke — and nothing else.

No tracking

Starling does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency, the advertising identifier (IDFA), or any third-party analytics or crash-reporting framework.

Children's privacy

Starling does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on our side. All data stays on your Mac under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
