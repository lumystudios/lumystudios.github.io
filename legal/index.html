Reporting a problem

To report a bug, a security issue, an inappropriate ad or content you believe infringes your rights, email lumystudios@yahoo.com with the app name and what you found. We read every message.

Accessibility

We want this website to work for everyone, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

  • Every page works with a keyboard, and focused links are clearly outlined.
  • Animations are turned off when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Screenshots have text descriptions, and text meets contrast guidelines.
  • The website works without JavaScript; it only adds animation.

If something is hard to use, email lumystudios@yahoo.com and tell us the page and the problem. We will fix it or send you the information in another way.
