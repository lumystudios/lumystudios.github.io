Little Wonders Privacy Policy

Little Wonders — Match & Build · Effective November 1, 2026

Little Wonders is a match-3 puzzle game developed by Udi Magen (“we”, “us”). There are no accounts, chat or social features, and we do not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or location.

Information stored on the device

  • Your levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, built worlds and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Deleting the app deletes this information.

Advertising

Little Wonders shows ads through Google AdMob, but only when you ask: in the Store you can choose to watch a short video for a gift. There are no ads between levels and no banners, and ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect device information such as the device type, operating-system version, IP address (used for approximate location) and ad interactions. Little Wonders does not ask to track you, so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps.

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Little Wonders shows Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choices later in the app under Settings › Privacy choices.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Coin packs, star packs and the starter bundle are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Apple, and keeps the transaction number on your device so a purchase is never delivered twice.

Email

If you write to us, for example with Contact us or Report an ad in Settings, we use your email address and message only to reply and to fix the problem.

Children

Little Wonders is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and information was collected, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our side apart from emails you send us. For data processed by Google to serve ads, use the in-app privacy choices or Google's privacy controls linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
